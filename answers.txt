What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)? List 2 of each.

Pros:
- This development methodology offers high flexibility.
- It enables quick delivery.

Cons:
- It lowers predictability since the end scope will most likely change from what was initially planned.
- Building on that, it enables the scope to increase in coverage without thorough discussion of timeline changes.

Explain the role and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.

A Scrum Master is responsible for maintaining Agile development practices and improving team efficiency/productivity, whereas a Product Owner is responsible for determining which features to build, why they should be built, and when they should be built.
