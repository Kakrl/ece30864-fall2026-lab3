What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)? List 2 of each.

Pros:
- This development methodology offers high flexibility.
- It enables quick delivery.

Cons:
- It lowers predictability since the end scope will most likely change from what was initially planned.
- Building on that, it enables the scope to increase in coverage without thorough discussion of timeline changes.



Explain the role and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.

A Scrum Master is responsible for maintaining Agile development practices and improving team efficiency/productivity, whereas a Product Owner is responsible for determining which features to build, why they should be built, and when they should be built.



What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle? List at least 2 points on what needs to be discussed during each of these meetings.

The 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle are Sprint Planning, Stand-up, and Sprint Review.

What is discussed:
Sprint Planning - overall goal of the upcoming sprint and determines what tasks within the backlog should be accomplished to reach that goal, with consideration of time.
Stand-up - what everyone has done since the last stand-up and what everyone plans to do next.
Sprint Review - if the goal of the sprint was achieved and how this sprint fits into the overall timeline.



Differentiate between Epic, Story, Bug, and Task issue types in JIRA

Epic - A large collection of work and usually spans multiple sprints. Has child tickets indicating smaller stories/tasks/bugs that make up the work necessary to be able to say a whole new version of a product, for example, is ready.
Story - Describes a requirement from the perspective of a user and is small enough to be completed in a sprint.
Bug - A known defect that needs fixing.
Task - Something that needs to be done. Anything that wouldn't fit well with the other categories goes here.
